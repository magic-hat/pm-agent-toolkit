# Customer feedback

What customers said and asked for: call notes, support themes, survey results and quotes.

## File names

`YYYY-MM-DD <customer or segment> - <topic>.md`.

## Front matter

Use the 7 fields in `AGENTS.md`. These rules apply in this folder:

- `status`: active or archived.
- `product`: required.
- `source`: call, ticket, survey or field team.

## Rules for this folder

- Keep the words of the customer separate from your interpretation.
- Write the problem that causes the request. Do not write only the feature that the customer asked for.
- Obey the rules of the user's company about customer data. If the rules do not permit a customer name in the vault, use a segment name.
